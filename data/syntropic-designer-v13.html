Fill Boundary from Keyline

Offsets your drawn keyline up- and down-slope at a fixed spacing to fill the boundary, then turns each offset line into a plantable row. Draw a keyline first with the ⌀ Keyline tool.

Fills the whole boundary. To fill only part of a field (multi-zone), draw a Fill Zone in the sidebar first, then turn Append on. In append mode new rows add to the set and near-duplicate rows are skipped; in replace mode rows are regenerated and plants re-link to the nearest new row.
